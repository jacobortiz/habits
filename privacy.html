Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

The short version. Habits has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. Your habits and check-ins live on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own private iCloud. Health data is read on your iPhone to check habits off for you and never leaves your devices. We never see any of it.

What the app stores

  • Habits, check-ins, rest days, and settings. Stored on your iPhone. Your Apple Watch keeps a copy of today's habits so you can check in from your wrist. Widgets, Siri Shortcuts, and Control Center read the same data on your device.
  • Reminders. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a notification server.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Habits syncs your habits and check-ins between your devices through Apple's CloudKit, in the private database of your iCloud account. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account and your iCloud terms. The developer cannot read, access, or recover it. Turning off iCloud for Habits in iOS Settings stops syncing; deleting the app's iCloud data in Settings removes it from iCloud.

Health data

You can link a habit to a Health metric such as steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes, sleep, water, workouts, or stand hours. When you do, and only after you grant permission in the Health permission sheet:

  • Habits reads that metric from the Health app on your iPhone to compute a daily total and check the habit off.
  • The daily totals are cached on your iPhone and shown on your paired Apple Watch. They are never written to Health, never synced to iCloud, and never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Health data is used only to show you your own progress. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or any other purpose, and never shared with third parties.

You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing > Apps > Habits, or by unlinking the habit in Habits. Unlinking deletes the cached totals.

On-device intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Habits can turn a sentence you type into a habit and write a short weekly note about your check-ins. Both run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device models. Nothing you type and none of your check-ins are sent anywhere. You can turn this off in Habits > Settings.

Purchases

Habits Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only that the purchase is valid. The developer never receives your name, payment details, or Apple Account information.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in. We do not know who you are.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app contains no third-party code that phones home.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data. There is nothing to sell.
  • No data about children is collected. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a habit in the app to remove it and all of its check-ins. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. If iCloud sync was on, remove the app's data from iCloud in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. The app's behavior described here will not change without the policy changing first.
