Habits for iPhone and Apple Watch

Habits tracks what you do at the cadence you actually keep: every day, certain days, every few days, or a few times a week. Miss a day and your progress stays. Health can check habits off for you, and everything runs on your own devices.

Questions people ask

Why didn't my steps fill in? Open the habit, make sure it is linked to a Health source, and check that Habits has read access in the Health app under Sharing > Apps. The first time, the Health permission sheet has two pages; both need to be completed.

Where is iCloud sync? It is automatic when you are signed in to iCloud. Habits > Settings shows its status.

What is Habits Pro? A one-time purchase that lifts the three-habit limit and adds Health auto-complete, timed sessions, on-device intelligence, the full icon catalog, and a custom day start. Restore it from Settings on any device signed in with the same Apple Account, or through Family Sharing.

How do I turn off the AI features? Habits > Settings > On-device intelligence. Nothing runs when it is off.

Night owl? Settings > Day starts at. A check-in at 1 AM can still count for the evening before.
